A landmark photographic exhibition at NGMA Fort celebrating the visual evolution of Indian cinema through rare film stills, archival studio portraits, and documentary photography curated by film historian SMM Ausaja. Marking Mumbai's UNESCO Creative City of Film recognition, the show includes tribute walls for Dharmendra and Asha Bhosle alongside works by photographers Pradeep Chandra, Sudharak Olwe, and Bandeep Singh.
