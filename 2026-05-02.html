India Post Payments Bank has launched a dedicated savings account for Self-Help Groups, offering zero-balance, zero-charge banking with doorstep delivery through its postal network. The product targets women-led rural SHGs, extending formal financial access to some of India's most underserved communities.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▲Rural SHG Women Members
Zero-balance accounts with doorstep banking eliminate travel costs and minimum-balance barriers, giving millions of rural women direct access to formal savings instruments.
▲India Post Payments Bank
SHG-dedicated accounts expand IPPB's deposit base and strengthen its financial inclusion mandate, leveraging the postman network as a low-cost last-mile distribution channel.
▼Regional Rural Banks and Co-operative Banks
IPPB's zero-charge SHG offering with superior postal reach may divert group deposits away from RRBs and co-operatives that currently serve rural SHG clusters.
