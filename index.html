Clearing credit card balances in full and keeping utilisation low produces faster credit score gains than reducing loans. Consistent EMI payments and steady loan repayment complement the strategy over the longer term.

LOW IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS LOW IMPACT
▲Aspiring Home Loan Borrowers
Lowering card utilisation and clearing dues before applying can quickly raise CIBIL scores, unlocking better loan terms.
▼Credit Card Holders Carrying Balances
Revolving unpaid balances keeps utilisation high, directly suppressing credit scores even when EMIs on other loans are paid on time.
