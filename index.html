Banks Reject Personal Loans Despite High Credit Scores

A credit score above 750 improves but does not guarantee personal loan approval, as lenders also weigh income stability, debt-to-income ratio, employer profile, and loan purpose. Applicants with strong scores still face rejection when other underwriting criteria fall short.

LOW IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS LOW IMPACT
▼Salaried Loan Applicants
Borrowers relying solely on a high credit score risk rejection if existing EMIs, job tenure, or employer category fail lender benchmarks.
◆Retail Banks and NBFCs
Multi-factor rejection practices, if poorly communicated, raise mis-selling complaints and push applicants toward costlier informal credit.

GST Unblocks Input Tax Credit on Corporate Insurance and Hospitality Services

Companies can now claim input tax credit on insurance premiums paid for employee or business cover under the GST framework. ITC is also extended to businesses that purchase and resell restaurant, catering, hotel, and fitness services within the same trade.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▲Corporate Employers
Companies can recover GST paid on group health and life insurance premiums, directly cutting the net cost of employee cover.
▲Event Managers and Travel Firms
Businesses that procure and resell catering, hotel bookings, and fitness services can now offset input GST against output liability, improving margins.
▲Hospitality and Catering Sector
Lower effective procurement costs for corporate buyers are likely to stimulate B2B demand for hotel rooms, restaurant services, and catering contracts.
