Borrowers juggling multiple credit card EMIs may reduce their interest burden by refinancing into a single personal loan, provided the loan rate is meaningfully lower than the blended card EMI rate. The strategy involves trade-offs around tenure, prepayment charges, and credit score impact.

Shifting from card EMI rates of 18–42% p.a. to personal loan rates of 10–15% can materially cut total interest paid and simplify monthly cash-flow management.
▼Credit Card Issuers
Customers refinancing EMI balances out of cards shrink the high-margin revolving book, directly eroding interest and processing-fee income.

India Post Payments Bank Launches Zero-Balance Accounts for Rural Self Help Groups

India Post Payments Bank has introduced a dedicated savings account for Self Help Groups, offering zero minimum balance, zero service charges, and doorstep banking via its postal network. The product is aimed primarily at women-led SHGs in rural and semi-urban India to bring their pooled funds into the formal banking system.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▲Women SHG Members
Zero-balance, doorstep accounts remove cost and travel barriers, enabling rural SHGs to safely park pooled savings in formal banking rather than informal channels.
▲Rural Microfinance Ecosystem
Formalising SHG savings weakens dependence on moneylenders and strengthens the credit history of groups that borrow from NABARD-linked schemes.
▲India Post Payments Bank
Dedicated SHG accounts expand IPPB's low-cost deposit base and differentiate it from bank correspondents and regional rural banks competing for the same rural segment.
