Banks Tighten Lounge Access Rules; Few Cards Still Offer Free Entry

Most banks have begun imposing minimum spending thresholds before credit card holders can access airport lounges for free. A small set of cards continues to offer unconditional complimentary lounge access, making card selection more consequential for frequent travellers.

Indian Equity Fund Inflows Fall 30% Even Without Market Crash

Indian equity mutual fund inflows have declined nearly 30% over the past two years despite the absence of a significant market downturn. The trend exposes a persistent behavioural gap between rational investment advice and retail investors' emotional response to periods of subdued returns.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▼SIP Investors
Cutting contributions during flat markets forfeits rupee-cost averaging benefits and long-term compounding, the very advantage systematic investing is designed to deliver.
▼Asset Management Companies
Shrinking equity inflows slow AUM growth and compress fee revenues at a time when competition among fund houses for retail money is already intense.
▼Financial Distributors and Advisors
Sustained inflow decline signals that investor education campaigns have failed to overcome the behavioural impulse to pull back when short-term returns disappoint, threatening distributor commission volumes.
