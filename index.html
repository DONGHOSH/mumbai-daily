Borrowers who settle a loan by paying less than the full outstanding amount receive a negative credit-bureau flag that can persist for years, limiting future borrowing. Rebuilding requires disciplined use of new credit lines, secured cards, and timely repayment behaviour.

Unpaid ₹50,000 Credit Card Bill Nearly Doubles Over 12 Months

An unpaid credit card balance of ₹50,000 compounds to roughly ₹80,153 in 12 months at the standard 3.5% monthly interest rate, inclusive of GST. The effective annual cost of revolving credit exceeds 42%, making minimum-payment habits financially damaging for cardholders.
