Heatwave-Ozone Combination Drove 830 Extra Cardiac Deaths in 2024

A peer-reviewed study found that surface ozone during Indian heatwaves exceeds the WHO guideline of 70 μg/m³ in every region of the country, reaching 85-110 μg/m³ across northern India. The combined heat-ozone stress contributed to approximately 830 additional cardiac deaths during the 2024 heatwave compared to the days immediately preceding it.

HIGH IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS HIGH IMPACT
▼Senior Citizens
Older adults face the sharpest cardiac mortality risk as ageing cardiovascular systems are least able to withstand simultaneous heat and ozone stress.
▼Outdoor Workers
Construction workers, agricultural labourers, and street vendors endure prolonged dual exposure to peak heat and ozone with no current occupational protection standard addressing the combined hazard.
▼Public Hospitals and Emergency Services
Heatwave periods now predictably spike cardiac emergencies, placing additional strain on ICU and casualty capacity in cities across northern and central India.
▼Centre and State Health Ministries
Study evidence compels revision of Heat Action Plans to incorporate real-time ozone monitoring and cardiac-risk alerts, closing a major gap in current policy.
