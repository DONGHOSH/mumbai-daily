New Tax Bill streamlines exemption conditions for offshore funds

India's new Income Tax Bill proposes simplified eligibility conditions for offshore funds seeking tax exemptions, easing compliance burdens for fund managers operating outside India. The change is aimed at removing ambiguities that have historically discouraged offshore fund managers from basing their operations in India.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▲Offshore Fund Managers
Cleaner exemption criteria reduce the compliance burden and legal uncertainty that previously deterred managers from pooling India-focused capital offshore.
▲GIFT City IFSC
Simpler rules make India's international financial hub more attractive for fund domiciliation, potentially drawing more foreign fund management activity to GIFT City.
▲Foreign Portfolio Investors
Reduced structural complexity lowers the cost and risk of routing capital through offshore vehicles that invest into Indian markets.
