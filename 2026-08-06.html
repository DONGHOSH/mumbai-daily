The RBI kept the repo rate unchanged, holding home loan interest rates steady and sustaining momentum in residential housing demand. Industry bodies CREDAI and NAREDCO welcomed the decision, citing improved buyer confidence and clearer project planning conditions for developers.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▲Prospective Home Buyers
Unchanged repo rate prevents floating-rate EMI increases, keeping housing affordability intact during an active residential sales cycle.
▲Real Estate Developers
Rate certainty reduces financing cost volatility, enabling developers to plan project launches, pricing, and construction timelines with greater confidence.
▲Housing Finance Companies and Banks
Stable policy rates support steady home loan disbursement growth without triggering repayment stress or EMI-linked delinquencies in existing borrower portfolios.
