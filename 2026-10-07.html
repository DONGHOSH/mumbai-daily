Checking Your Own Credit Report Does Not Dent Your Score

Self-initiated credit report checks are classified as soft inquiries and have no effect on credit scores, unlike lender-initiated hard inquiries which can cause a small dip. Regular self-monitoring helps consumers identify reporting errors and detect fraudulent credit activity early.

LOW IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS LOW IMPACT
▲First-time Borrowers
Clarifying that self-checks are soft inquiries removes hesitancy and encourages proactive credit monitoring to catch errors before loan applications.
▼Loan Applicants
Multiple hard inquiries from lenders within a short window can cumulatively lower scores, making the timing and sequencing of loan applications strategically important.
