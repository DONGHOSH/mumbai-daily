The proposed rules would fundamentally narrow the product range non-bank lenders can offer borrowers.

HIGH IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS HIGH IMPACT
▼NBFCs
Must discontinue revolving credit portfolios and restructure product offerings, directly hitting fee income and growth strategies built around flexible credit.
▼MSME Borrowers
Lose access to NBFC-provided revolving working capital lines, forcing a shift to bank credit that many small businesses cannot qualify for.
▼Fintech Lending Platforms
Fintechs using NBFC licenses or NBFC partnerships to power 'credit line' and BNPL revolving products face direct business model disruption under the new rules.
▼Retail Borrowers on Credit Lines
Consumers relying on NBFC-issued personal credit lines for flexible drawdowns must move to credit cards or bank overdrafts, typically less accessible or more expensive.

NBFCs Post 14.4% Credit Growth, Retail and Farm Loans Lead

NBFC credit rose 14.4% year-on-year to ₹59.3 lakh crore in June, with retail loans growing fastest at 20.3%, driven by housing, vehicle, and gold jewellery segments. Agriculture credit expanded 17.9% and services 17.6%, while credit to industry lagged at 6.7%.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▲Home Buyers & Vehicle Buyers
Surging NBFC retail lending broadens credit access for borrowers underserved by banks, especially in semi-urban and Tier-2 markets.
▲Gold Loan Borrowers
Robust gold jewellery loan growth signals NBFCs are deepening credit reach to informal-sector households and small traders using gold as collateral.
▼RBI & Financial Regulators
Double-digit retail credit expansion at NBFCs, outpacing bank lending, increases systemic exposure and may prompt tighter oversight of underwriting standards and concentration risk.

RBI Draft Rules Threaten NBFC Flexi and Overdraft Loan Products

The RBI has proposed draft directions restricting revolving credit facilities offered by NBFCs, which could force a restructuring of flexi loans and overdraft products across corporate, MSME, and unsecured personal loan segments. Morgan Stanley flagged the potential disruption to NBFC loan book structures in a research note.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▼MSME Borrowers
MSMEs using NBFC flexi and overdraft lines for day-to-day working capital may lose access to revolving facilities, pushing them toward costlier, less flexible fixed-term loans.
▼NBFCs with Flexi Loan Books
Lenders such as Bajaj Finance and IIFL Finance face mandatory product redesign, risking a contraction in revolving credit portfolios and a dip in processing fee income.
▼Unsecured Personal Loan Borrowers
Retail customers who tap flexi credit lines for on-demand withdrawals may see these products discontinued or converted into standard fixed-instalment loans with less repayment flexibility.
