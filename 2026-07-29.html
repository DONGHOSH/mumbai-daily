BUSINESS LINE · Tue, 28 Jul 2026

India's Fintech Sector Posts First Aggregate Profit in FY25

India's fintech industry turned profitable in FY25 for the first time, with aggregate PAT swinging from a ₹116 billion loss in FY22 to a ₹23 billion profit, according to Jefferies. Sector revenue grew nearly five-fold to ₹1,033 billion in FY25 and is projected to reach ₹2,396 billion by FY30 at an 18% CAGR.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
◆Fintech Founders & Management
Business models face a structural shift from growth-at-any-cost to margin discipline, rewarding operationally efficient players and squeezing the rest.
▲VC and PE Investors in Fintech
Aggregate profitability improves exit visibility and IPO readiness across portfolios, supporting better valuations after years of write-downs.
▼Digital Lending and Payments Users
As fintechs prioritise margins over acquisition, subsidised cashbacks, zero-fee products, and below-market credit rates are likely to be gradually withdrawn.
