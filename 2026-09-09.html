HIGH IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS HIGH IMPACT
▲Indian Diaspora & Tourists Abroad
Wider UPI acceptance in more countries lets Indians transact in local currencies without cash or costly forex cards.
▲Indian Exporters & MSMEs
Cross-border UPI settlements cut transaction fees and speed up payment collection from overseas buyers significantly.
▲NPCI
Global expansion boosts transaction volumes and opens licensing and interchange revenue streams from partner-country deployments.
▼Visa & Mastercard
UPI's state-backed international push directly competes in high-growth corridors — South Asia, Gulf, Southeast Asia — where both networks earn premium cross-border fees.
