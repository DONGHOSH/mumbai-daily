DBS Bank India is financing a commercial tower by K Raheja Corp in Pune with a Rs 520 crore credit facility. Separately, Certus Capital has deployed over Rs 1,000 crore into real estate projects across Hyderabad, Mumbai, Chennai, and the NCR in the first half of 2026.
