Zerodha Lifts Instant Withdrawal Cap to ₹5 Lakh, Extends Weekend Access

Zerodha has raised its daily instant withdrawal limit from ₹2 lakh to ₹5 lakh. The facility now operates from 9 am to 6 pm on weekdays, weekends, and market holidays, giving retail traders faster access to funds in their trading accounts.

Neo Closes ₹6,200-Crore Second Private Credit Fund

Neo Asset Management has raised ₹6,200 crore through its second private credit fund, drawing commitments from family offices and institutional investors. The close signals continued strong appetite for private credit as an alternative to traditional bank lending in India.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▲Mid-Market Borrowers
Companies too large for microfinance but underserved by banks gain access to a fresh ₹6,200 crore pool of non-bank credit capital.
▲Family Offices & HNI Investors
Participating family offices lock into private credit yields that typically exceed public fixed-income returns, diversifying away from equities and real estate.
▲Indian Private Credit Sector
A second, larger fund close by Neo validates the maturing private credit ecosystem and intensifies competition with established players like Ares, Kotak Alternatives, and Blackstone Credit.
