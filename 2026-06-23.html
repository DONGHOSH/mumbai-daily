SEBI Overhauls Exchange Rules, Replaces Multiple Circulars With Single Framework

SEBI is proposing a comprehensive regulatory overhaul for stock exchanges and clearing corporations, consolidating overlapping circulars into one master circular and scrapping obsolete registration norms. The reform targets reduced compliance burden and a more streamlined operational framework for market infrastructure institutions.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▲Stock Exchanges (NSE, BSE, MCX)
A single master circular replaces a patchwork of directives, cutting legal review costs and reducing the risk of inadvertent regulatory breaches.
▲Clearing Corporations (NSCCL, ICCL)
Revised and rationalised reporting requirements lower administrative overhead, freeing operational bandwidth currently spent on redundant filings.
▲Market Intermediaries and Brokers
Discontinuation of outdated registration norms simplifies onboarding and renewal processes, reducing compliance timelines for entities seeking exchange membership.
