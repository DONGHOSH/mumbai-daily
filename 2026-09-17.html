MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▼Retail Investors on Digital Platforms
MDR levied on UPI-based investment transactions would raise per-transaction costs, eroding returns on small-ticket SIPs and stock purchases.
▼Digital Broking Startups (INDmoney, Groww, Zerodha)
Platforms handling hundreds of crores in daily UPI flows would face crore-scale incremental costs, squeezing already thin unit economics and potentially forcing fee hikes.
▼UPI Ecosystem / NPCI
Charging MDR on investment-linked UPI transactions risks discouraging first-time retail investors from using digital channels, slowing financialisation of savings.
