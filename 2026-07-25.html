CreditAccess Grameen's net profit jumped nearly five-fold to Rs 493 crore in Q1, driven by a 63% year-on-year fall in credit costs and improved borrower repayments. Assets under management grew 16.4% as the company added new customers.

Bank Credit Growth Eases to 17.7% as Deposit Pace Slows

Bank credit grew 17.7% year-on-year in the fortnight ending July 15, outpacing deposit growth of 12.7% as outstanding credit reached Rs 217.3 lakh crore against Rs 262.9 lakh crore in deposits. Corporate borrowing remained the primary engine of credit demand.
