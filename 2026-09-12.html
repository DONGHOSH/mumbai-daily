Unified Digital Layer Proposed to Plug India's Rs 60 Lakh Crore MSME Credit Gap

A new report recommends building a centralised interoperable digital infrastructure connecting small businesses, banks, and payment providers to address India's vast MSME formal credit gap. The system would digitise the order-to-cash cycle, automate invoice reconciliation, and enable cash-flow-based lending rather than collateral-dependent underwriting.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▲Small Business Owners (MSMEs)
Cash-flow-based underwriting would let asset-light small businesses qualify for formal credit using invoice and transaction data instead of collateral.
▲Banks and NBFCs
Standardised digital receivables data would sharply cut credit-assessment costs and default risk on MSME loan books, unlocking a vast underserved market.
▲Fintech and Payment Aggregators
Mandatory interoperability in the proposed layer would embed payment platforms as core nodes in MSME credit flows, expanding their role beyond transactions.
