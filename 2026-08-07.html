Fisdom co-founders Subramanya S.V. and Anand Dalmia are departing Groww roughly one year after the $150-million acquisition closed, completing their post-deal retention period. Their exit marks the end of the founding team's formal involvement in the integrated wealth-tech platform.

Super.money launches instalment credit product for first-time borrowers

Super.money has introduced SplitStore, a closed-loop pay-in-three credit product aimed at consumers with no prior credit history. The small-ticket offering is designed to bring new-to-credit users into formal credit ecosystems through commerce.
