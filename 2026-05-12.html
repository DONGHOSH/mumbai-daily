Peak XV, Sequoia Offload Rs 4,750 Crore Groww Stake via Block Deals

Early investors including Peak XV, Sequoia Capital, and Ribbit Capital are exiting Groww shares worth Rs 4,750 crore through block deals at a floor price of Rs 177 per share. The announcement triggered a sharp fall in Groww's stock as markets reacted to the scale of institutional selling.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▼Groww Retail Shareholders
Block deal discount sets a new lower price anchor, directly eroding portfolios of investors who bought shares at or above listing price.
▼Groww ESOP Holders
Falling stock price compresses the realizable value of employee stock options, reducing a key part of compensation for Groww's workforce.
▲Peak XV, Sequoia Capital, Ribbit Capital
Block deal enables a profitable liquidity event on a marquee fintech bet, returning capital to limited partners after a multi-year holding period.

Kamath Proposes Gold-Backed Stablecoin to Reduce India's Dollar Dependence

Zerodha co-founder Nikhil Kamath has called for India to explore a gold-linked stablecoin as an alternative to dollar-backed digital currencies. He argues the model could mobilise the country's vast idle household gold reserves while reducing reliance on dollar-dominated digital financial infrastructure.

LOW IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS LOW IMPACT
◆Indian Household Gold Owners
An estimated 25,000 tonnes of idle household gold could theoretically be digitised and monetised, but only if a credible regulatory and custody framework is built around such a stablecoin.
▼RBI and Crypto Policymakers
Any serious move toward a gold-backed stablecoin would require the RBI to revisit its cautious stance on private digital currencies and design an entirely new oversight regime.
