Eight large-cap BSE 100 stocks including Wipro, Hindustan Unilever, ONGC, and PB Fintech touched fresh 52-week lows as the Sensex slipped 49 points to 72,480. The declines, some as steep as 45% in a month, span IT, FMCG, energy, and financial services sectors.
