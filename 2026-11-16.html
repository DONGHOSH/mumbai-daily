When a borrower misses EMI payments, the loan guarantor's credit score is equally damaged, since lenders treat guarantors as co-obligants and report defaults on their credit records. Guarantors also carry full repayment liability if the primary borrower fails, making the decision to sign a guarantee agreement a significant financial risk.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
▼Loan Guarantors
A borrower's missed EMIs are reported against the guarantor's credit file, cutting their score and blocking their own future loan approvals.
▼Prospective Guarantors
People pressured by family or friends to co-sign loans face contingent liability that lenders count against their own borrowing capacity, often without realising it.
▲Banks and NBFCs
Lenders gain a stronger recovery channel when guarantors understand their legal liability, reducing the risk of prolonged bad-loan disputes on retail and education loans.
