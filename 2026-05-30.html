PB Fintech co-founders Yashish Dahiya and Alok Bansal offloaded 38 lakh shares worth Rs 665 crore through a block deal. Goldman Sachs and Tata Mutual Fund were among the institutional investors that absorbed the stake.

MEDIUM IMPACT
IMPACT ANALYSIS MEDIUM IMPACT
◆PB Fintech Retail Shareholders
A large founder stake sale typically pressures the stock short-term, though marquee institutional absorption at block price provides a meaningful demand buffer.
▲Institutional Fund Managers (Goldman Sachs, Tata MF)
Block deal entry gives large funds a structured route into PB Fintech's insurtech growth story, likely at a negotiated discount to market price.
▲PB Fintech Promoters
Founders partially monetise holdings to diversify personal wealth while retaining a substantial enough combined stake to signal continued operational commitment.
