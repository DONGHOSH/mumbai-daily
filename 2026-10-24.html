India's Bank Deposits Shed ₹5.19 Lakh Crore in a Fortnight

Indian scheduled commercial banks recorded a ₹5.19 lakh crore drop in deposits and a ₹2.52 lakh crore contraction in credit outstanding in the fortnight ended October 15. Year-on-year deposit growth eased to 16.7% and credit growth slowed to 18.2%, continuing a moderating trend.

Tightening credit outstanding raises refinancing risk for working-capital-dependent businesses relying on short-cycle bank rollovers.

Simultaneous moderation in both deposit and credit growth gives policymakers cover to hold rates steady without stoking excess credit expansion.

Westside Charges ₹49 Credit Card Fee; Legality Under Scrutiny

Westside is levying a flat ₹49 surcharge on all credit card transactions, passing on part of its merchant discount rate cost to customers. Legal experts say RBI rules clearly prohibit such surcharges on debit cards but the framework for credit cards remains ambiguous.
